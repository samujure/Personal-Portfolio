research.

interpretability, mostly. whether the reasoning a model writes down is the reasoning it uses.

Paper, in preparation

Does CoT Causally Affect Model Behavior?

Srivamsi Amujure*, co-authors
*Equal contribution. Work done at Algoverse, advised by Darpan Aswal (Université Grenoble Alpes).

Planned submission: EACL 2027 Student Research Workshop (non-archival).

Language models often write out a chain of thought before they answer. We ask whether that chain of thought causally shapes the final answer, or is produced alongside it without driving it. We intervene on the reasoning across several models and measure whether, and by how much, the answer changes.
